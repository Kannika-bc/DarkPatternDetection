How to pick a good pair of earbuds

Start with fit. Earbuds that do not seal well will sound thin no matter how good the drivers are. Most models ship with three sizes of tips; try all of them.

Next, look at battery life with noise cancellation switched on, not the headline number. Finally, check which codecs your phone supports before paying extra for one.
