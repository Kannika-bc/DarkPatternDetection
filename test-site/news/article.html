City lakes get a second life as volunteers restore wetlands

Over the past two years, groups of volunteers have cleared weeds, rebuilt inlets and planted native grasses around three lakes on the city's eastern edge. Birdwatchers say migratory species have returned for the first time in a decade.

Officials credit a mix of citizen effort and a small municipal grant. "The lakes were dying slowly," said one volunteer coordinator. "Now the water is clear enough to see the bottom near the inlets."
